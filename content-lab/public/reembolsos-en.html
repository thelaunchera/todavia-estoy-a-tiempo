If you cannot access Content Lab

Use the email address used for your purchase. If access does not appear or you never receive an email, contact us so we can verify the payment and fix delivery. You do not need to purchase again to request help.

If there is a problem with your purchase

If a purchased feature is not available or has a substantial defect, we will review your purchase and the issue to correct it or assess an appropriate refund. Making a request does not mean automatic approval and does not remove mandatory buyer rights.

Duplicate or unrecognized charges

Contact us with the purchase date and email address so we can review the history. Do not share passwords, access codes or complete card details. You may also contact your payment provider through its usual channels.

What this purchase does not include

Content Lab uses one-time pricing, so there is no Content Lab monthly subscription to cancel. Payments made directly to outside tools, such as ChatGPT, must be managed with those providers. Your purchase includes visual design, planning, idea libraries and prompts. It does not include AI image generation, video editing, automatic publishing or third-party subscriptions.

How to request a review

Send a message to hello@thelaunchera.com with Content Lab in the subject, your purchase email, and a brief explanation. We will review the offer, access and use relevant to the problem. Change-of-mind requests are assessed under the offer terms and applicable law. If a refund is due, it is issued through the original payment method; posting time depends on the provider.
