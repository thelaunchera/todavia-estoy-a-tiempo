Content Lab Privacy

How your information is used when you create, save and organize content in Content Lab.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Information you use in Content Lab

We process your account email, purchase reference and the information you choose to save: brand profiles, optional social channels, photos, logos, references, designs, text and planned posting dates. You do not need to share social media passwords to plan content.

How we use it

We use this information to verify access, save projects, display files, process requested features and respond to inquiries. Files belong to your account and brand. Content shared by the administration is kept distinct from your personal files; uploading a personal photo does not make it a public resource.

Services involved

Supabase stores access and files; Stripe processes payments; Make supports delivery; and Resend sends emails. We do not ask you to send full card numbers. The idea bank and prompts let you copy instructions into outside tools; copying text does not automatically send your photos. If you use the optional AI carousel-copy feature, the topic and necessary brand context may be sent to the provider processing the request; this does not generate photos. If you upload files directly to ChatGPT or another service, that service's policies apply.

Free preview and ChatGPT

In the public prompt preview, your selected photo is used in your browser to prepare instructions. Copying a prompt does not send your photo to ChatGPT by itself. If you later upload files to an external tool, its policies govern the processing.

Sessions, retention and requests

Your browser stores information needed to maintain a session and remember activity. Projects are saved so you can edit them later. You may ask to access, correct or delete information by contacting hello@thelaunchera.com using your account email. We may need to retain purchase or security records where required by law or a legitimate need, and we will explain this when responding. We do not promise that external providers retain no data.

Contact

The Launch Era is the business responsible for Content Lab. For privacy and file questions, contact hello@thelaunchera.com.
