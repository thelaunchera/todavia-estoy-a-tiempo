Refunds and Purchase Issues

How we review digital downloads, classes, subscriptions and services not delivered as agreed.

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Scope: Product Center and purchases linked from it

1. Before requesting a refund

Contact us with the product, purchase email, approximate date and explanation. Do not send full card details. We will review the transaction, what was delivered and the specific terms shown when you paid. Submitting a request does not guarantee automatic approval, and this policy does not remove rights provided by law.

2. PDFs and downloadable products

Once a digital file has been supplied and downloaded as described, change-of-mind refunds are generally not available where the law permits. If the file is damaged, incorrect, not as described or not delivered, contact us so we can correct it or consider an appropriate refund. Mandatory consumer rights take precedence.

3. Training and classroom access

A class shown as closed or awaiting launch does not promise immediate access. The policy and any advance-purchase refund conditions must be clearly stated in the offer and checkout. If a product is not made available under the promised conditions, we will review the issue and the legally appropriate remedy. Simply signing in does not prove that an entire course has been delivered.

4. Content Lab and software licenses

Content Lab access is verified by product and account. If a paid feature is not enabled or there is a major failure, contact support so we can check the purchase and fix access. Change-of-mind and later-use requests are governed by the specific offer terms where legally permissible. Licenses or subscriptions charged directly by external services are not refunded by The Launch Era.

5. Custom services and installations

Where research, design, configuration, human work or installation is involved, refund eligibility depends on work completed, deliverables and the proposal accepted before payment. If work has not started, request a review promptly. If part of the service was not provided or differed materially from the agreement, we will consider correction or the appropriate amount, subject to the contract and law. We do not state that all deposits are automatically non-refundable.

6. Fees, renewals and unfamiliar charges

Cancellation prevents future renewals as of its effective date, but does not automatically reverse an already billed period. Report duplicate or unfamiliar charges promptly so we can review and resolve them. You keep the right to contact your card issuer or payment provider through its available channels.

7. Request and response

Email hello@thelaunchera.com to begin a review. We will respond to the address provided with next steps and, where applicable, details needed to issue a refund to the original payment method. Final posting times depend on the payment provider.

Need help?

Write from the email used for your purchase. Include the product and a short explanation, and do not send passwords or complete card numbers. Contact: hello@thelaunchera.com.
