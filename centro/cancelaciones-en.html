Cancellations and Subscriptions

How to stop renewals and manage subscriptions and recurring business services.

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Scope: Product Center and purchases linked from it

1. Which products can have monthly fees?

Some AI assistants, support services and tools may be sold with an initial setup fee and recurring payments. Purchasing a PDF or one-time masterclass does not create a monthly subscription. The current price, billing cycle, first renewal and conditions must be displayed at the applicable checkout.

2. Requesting cancellation

Email hello@thelaunchera.com from your purchase email, stating the product and that you wish to stop renewals. If the checkout provider offers an official management or cancellation link in your receipt, you can use that channel as well. We do not ask you to share passwords or card numbers.

We will acknowledge the request and verify the account when needed. We will tell you the effective date and status of upcoming renewals. Send your request before the next billing date to avoid another charge; if you have already been charged, the payment will be reviewed under the refund policy and applicable law. There is no extra fee for asking to cancel.

3. What happens when you cancel

Stopping future renewals does not necessarily refund a billing period already started or work and setup legitimately provided. Access may remain active until the end of the paid period, depending on the offer and service security. Cancellation does not automatically erase documents, invoices or the account; you can request changes to your data separately using Your Data.

4. Other requests

If you want to change plans, prevent renewal after a promotion, dispute a charge or end a service project that has not started, use the same business contact. Simply stopping use of a tool does not count as a cancellation request.

Need help?

Write from the email used for your purchase. Include the product and a short explanation, and do not send passwords or complete card numbers. Contact: hello@thelaunchera.com.
