Product Center Privacy

How we use business information, the providers involved and your privacy options.

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Scope: the Product Center and linked purchases

1. Responsibility and contact

The Launch Era operates this Product Center and the related access systems. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact hello@thelaunchera.com. A service purchased through thelaunchera.com may also be covered by the privacy policy provided in that purchase flow.

2. Information we process

Card payments are processed by the relevant payment provider. The Product Center does not need your full card number. Do not send passwords, full card numbers or sensitive information in emails or forms.

3. Why we use information

To validate payments, enable product-specific access, deliver files, answer questions, prevent fraud, operate the purchased tools, meet legal obligations and send operational messages about purchases or security. Promotional messages and waitlists are managed separately from transactional messages and purchases, according to your preferences and applicable legal requirements.

4. Providers involved

To provide products, we may share strictly necessary information with providers responsible for payment processing, hosting, account authentication, email delivery, file delivery, support and content playback. Each provider processes information according to its function and relevant terms. Some may process data outside your state or country. If you use an external payment or content platform, its privacy and cookie notices also apply.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. Promotional messages require an appropriate permission or legal basis.

5. Security and retention

We use appropriate security measures and access restrictions to protect user information. We retain data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide purchased services, maintain accounts, respond to requests, prevent misuse and comply with legal and accounting obligations. Some receipts may be retained for legal requirements or disputes; where appropriate, information is deleted or anonymized.

6. Rights and preferences

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion and learn about your marketing communication options. We may verify identity before sharing or deleting data; legal exceptions may permit retention. To request assistance, use Your Data or Business Contact. You may manage optional statistics in Cookies; declining them does not prevent purchases or product access.

7. Children, third parties and security

The Product Center is intended for adults. We do not knowingly request information from children under 13. If a business customer shares customer information to configure a service, they must have the legal basis, permissions and notices required. We apply reasonable protection measures, though no connected system can guarantee absolute security. Report misuse to hello@thelaunchera.com.

Need help?

Contact hello@thelaunchera.com using the purchase email. Include the product and a brief explanation. Do not send passwords or complete card numbers.
