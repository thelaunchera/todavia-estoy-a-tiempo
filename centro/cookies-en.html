Cookies and Browser Storage

Which browser information supports access and which analytics are optional.

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Scope: Product Center and purchases linked from it

1. Required storage

The Product Center uses browser storage and similar technologies to maintain authentication sessions, verify a remembered device (up to 24 hours), manage password recovery, security and privacy preferences. Disabling these essential functions may prevent sign-in.

2. Optional analytics

On podcast-managed pages, optional first-party analytics should only run with your permission in our privacy preferences. Declining analytics does not block purchases or access. Preferences are stored for up to 180 days in that browser and may be changed at any time; browser privacy signals may also limit optional measurement.

3. Checkout and external services

External payment providers, video providers and other tools may use cookies or storage under their own notices. When you leave the Product Center for a The Launch Era checkout or payment platform, review that destination's controls and terms.

4. Changing preferences

Use this site's settings controls and your browser's options to remove certain stored information. Clearing authentication storage may sign you out and require you to sign in again. Deleting cookies does not cancel a subscription or erase your purchase history.
