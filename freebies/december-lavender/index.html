ChatGPT Prompts for Product Visuals

Five ready-to-use prompts for premium product photography. Use your own product reference and create one image at a time.

Attach your original product photo to ChatGPT before using any prompt. Replace the words in [BRACKETS] with your own product and brand details. AI-generated examples are illustrative, not real product photography.

Sculpted Gloss Hero

Create an editorial beauty campaign photograph using my attached product reference: [PRODUCT]. Keep its real silhouette, color, closure, materials and label placement consistent with the reference. Place one product on a softly reflective ivory surface next to a single sculptural ribbon of translucent [PRODUCT COLOR] gel or gloss. Use pale lavender as the supporting palette, indirect daylight, a gentle edge light and rich, believable material reflections. Compose from a three-quarter angle with one clear focal point and generous negative space for optional copy that will be added later. The result must resemble premium product photography, with realistic liquid viscosity, subtle imperfections, sharp packaging detail and soft shadows. Vertical 4:5, no extra products, no fake labels, no watermark and no text overlay.

Crea una fotografía editorial publicitaria usando mi referencia adjunta de [PRODUCTO]. Conserva la silueta real, color, tapa, materiales y ubicación de la etiqueta. Coloca una sola unidad sobre una superficie marfil con reflejo suave, al lado de una cinta escultórica de gel o gloss translúcido en [COLOR DEL PRODUCTO]. Usa lavanda pálido como apoyo, luz natural indirecta, luz de contorno delicada y reflejos materiales creíbles. Fotografía desde tres cuartos, con un protagonista claro y espacio negativo para añadir texto después. Busca realismo comercial premium: viscosidad auténtica, imperfecciones sutiles, empaque nítido y sombras suaves. Formato vertical 4:5; sin productos duplicados, etiquetas inventadas, marcas de agua ni texto añadido.

Droplet & Material Macro

Photograph a macro material study for [PRODUCT], guided by the attached reference photo. Make the hero a single clear droplet or transparent product swatch suspended on polished glass, with realistic surface tension, minute bubbles and restrained highlights. Add a softly blurred view of the real product behind the material only if it remains recognizable and does not create a false package. Art direction: muted lavender, cool ivory, controlled studio caustics, subtle shadows and believable depth of field. Use a close, tactile perspective that feels like a beauty magazine detail page, not abstract 3D art. Keep the composition quiet and sophisticated with a single visual story: how the formula feels. Vertical 4:5, photorealistic, no floating typography, duplicate containers, fake certification marks or watermark.

Fotografía un estudio macro de materiales para [PRODUCTO] a partir de la foto de referencia adjunta. El protagonista es una gota transparente o una muestra del producto sobre cristal pulido, con tensión superficial real, pequeñas burbujas y reflejos controlados. Puedes incluir el producto real desenfocado detrás solo si sigue siendo reconocible y no altera su envase. Dirección de arte en lavanda apagado y marfil frío, refracciones de estudio suaves, sombras delicadas y profundidad de campo creíble. La perspectiva debe ser cercana y táctil, como una página de detalle de una revista de belleza, no arte 3D abstracto. Una historia visual: cómo se siente la fórmula. Vertical 4:5, fotorrealista, sin textos, envases duplicados, sellos inventados ni marcas de agua.

Make the scene feel intimate, clean and tactile, like a believable beauty editorial shot. Prioritize the container's real shape, accurate material appearance and realistic contact shadows. Vertical 4:5, uncluttered composition, no false claims, no invented logo, no hands unless supported by the reference, no text or watermark.

Holiday Gift Campaign

The product must remain the hero, completely visible and true to its original packaging; the gift box is only a supporting detail. Aim for editorial holiday advertising rather than festive clip art or a generic Christmas template. Vertical 4:5, high realism, no added pricing, fake discount, copied logo, invented lettering, duplicate products or watermark.

Necesitamos pliegues y texturas creíbles, estilismo premium y espacio para respirar. El producto debe seguir siendo el protagonista, completo y fiel a su empaque; la caja es secundaria.
