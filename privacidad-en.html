Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who manages this space

Todavía estoy a tiempo is an editorial project offering episodes, resources and digital products. For inquiries, use the podcast help form.

What we collect and why

We use your email to deliver a free guide you request. If you join the “Your First AI Website” waitlist, we request separate permission and email confirmation, and use that signup only to send class-related updates. You can withdraw through the class or ask for removal through Help. Episode updates require a separate opt-in. When you contact us, we use the name, email, topic and message you provide to reply. Do not include sensitive information or passwords. Asking for help or buying a product never automatically subscribes you to marketing.

Purchases, access and external providers

Payments are handled by payment providers; the site does not require your full card number. A class or tool may require email-and-password access. We retain information needed to verify purchases, deliver materials, respond to questions and let you continue lessons. Hosting, authentication, email, file delivery and content providers may process technical or usage data needed for their services. External sites have their own privacy and cookie policies. Promotional messages require applicable permission.

Song analytics

Only with your permission do we collect song-start and playback-progress events using random session and playback identifiers. These events do not contain your name or email. They are not completely anonymous because the provider receives technical connection information. Rejecting analytics does not stop the podcast or the song. You can update your choice under cookies and storage.

Retention and requests

We retain data as needed for replies, delivery, requested subscriptions and purchase or security obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion and unsubscribe from updates.

Children and security

This reflection space is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit their data. If you believe a child's information was received, contact support. Technical controls and private access are applied, though no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Product Center data

The Product Center supports email-and-password access and a 24-hour remember-device option. Creating an account does not establish a purchase; access depends on the offer and availability. For Product Center purchases, support, access logs, Content Lab and business subscriptions, also review the business privacy notice and contact details.
