QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Let's answer your questions.

Here are answers to common questions.

Do I need to own a business?

No. You can practice with your own idea. If you decide to publish a real offer, you'll need to check the details, permissions and terms.

Can I build websites for other businesses?

Yes. You can practice with businesses like salons, cafés or cleaning services. You may eventually offer website building as a service, but finding clients takes time and practice.

Do I need to pay for other tools?

We'll start with free options and explore what each account allows. Some tools have limits or paid plans. I'll explain which ones are optional before you need them.

Do I need technical experience?

No. We start from zero with clear explanations and hands-on exercises.

What if I only have a phone?

Your phone is enough to get started. I'll show you how to work in your browser, build the practice project, open the URL and review responsive layouts. Some previews are simulated, and we'll also check how it works on your own phone.

What if I've never built a website?

This class starts from scratch. We'll organize your idea, prepare images and copy, use prompts and test your site on mobile. No coding or final business plan required.

Could this help me earn money?

Learning to build websites can help you offer a service or present your products better. But a website alone does not automatically bring sales; you still need a real offer, promotion and follow-up. This is a skill that may lead to income with practice and consistency, not a quick-money promise.

Can I copy the class prompts?

Yes. You'll get 12 core prompts plus additional classroom resources, organized so you know when to use each one.

Where can I watch lessons and find materials?

Your lessons and materials will be in a dedicated student space, linked to the email you use to purchase.

Will I learn about payments?

We'll look at an example showing products and reviewing the payment flow. For real sales, you will need to set up your own payment account.

How much is the class?

The launch price will be $9.99, one-time payment, for the first 15 days after opening. Then it will be $39.99. The waitlist is free.

Can I learn in English?

Yes. The classroom and written materials have English and Spanish versions. Video audio and subtitles will be labeled as they become available. Switching this page to English does not change the audio of a video.
