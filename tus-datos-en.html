Your Data and Your Choices

Updated: October 31, 2026

Access, correction or deletion

You can request a review of your information through the podcast help form. Select the privacy topic, if available, or explain your request in the message. Use the email address connected to your resource request or purchase. Only share what's needed to identify your request.

What happens next

The request goes to the podcast team for review. We may verify that the email and information belong to you before acting. Opening this page does not automatically delete a purchase, account or record. Some receipts and records may be retained to satisfy legal, transactional or security obligations.

Updates and statistics

Use the unsubscribe page to stop episode updates. To stop optional statistics, open Cookie Settings. These are separate choices; you do not have to delete a purchase to refuse analytics.

Business purchases and Product Center information

If your request involves your account, access, purchase, Content Lab or business services in the Product Center, email hello@thelaunchera.com. The business may verify your identity before disclosing, correcting or deleting information. Records required by law may be retained. Review the Product Center privacy notice.
