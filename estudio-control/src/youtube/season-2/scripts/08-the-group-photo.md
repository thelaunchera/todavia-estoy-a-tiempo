# SEASON 02 · E08 — THE GROUP PHOTO
**Series:** OFF BRIEF — THE CREATIVE HOUSE
**Setting:** Studio after launch, photography corner, closing dinner
**Featured cast:** NIA, RUBY, VAL, SAGE, NOVA, JULES, IMANI, MATEO, SIMONE, JUNE, ARDEN (message)
**Target length:** 6–8 minutes including repeated photo attempts, emotional pauses and a season-hook tag
**Status:** Full dialogue draft v1 · eight scenes · production not started

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: ONE NORMAL PHOTO
**SHOT:** Full-team portrait setup in front of the studio backdrop. Val holds the remote shutter.
**SFX:** Self-timer beeps. CLICK.
VAL: Someone blinked.
**ACTION:** Everyone resets. CLICK. Mateo is half outside the frame.
MATEO: I was edited out in real life.
**ACTION:** Reset. CLICK. Ruby loses her balance on a folding chair; Jules catches her.
NIA: ONE. NORMAL. PHOTO.
**ACTION:** The entire room looks directly at camera, suddenly perfectly still.
**CLICK. CUT TO:** OFF BRIEF title on a camera flash.

### SCENE 2 — THE DAY AFTER
**SHOT:** Warm quiet studio, decorated with a few leftover launch ribbons. Nia tidies and notices old season-one props.
NIA: The launch is over. We should reset before the next project.
RUBY: Reset what?
NIA: Set storage. Expenses. Freelance schedules. The massive table that's eating our floor.
RUBY: That's our family table.
NIA: It's a production table.
RUBY: The table has watched us grow.
NIA: It has blocked the door twice.
RUBY: Character development.
**ACTION:** Nova walks in carrying a hard drive.
NOVA: The campaign footage is backed up in two places.
NIA: Thank you.
NOVA: This studio is the only place where saying that makes someone emotional.
**ACTION:** Nia starts to smile, then looks at the empty chairs.

### SCENE 3 — WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?
**SHOT:** Nia sets a single printed next-quarter plan on the table. Ruby sits across from her.
NIA: We can go back to the two of us for smaller jobs.
RUBY: We could.
NIA: It'd be simpler.
RUBY: Was simpler actually better?
NIA: We used to make decisions in ten minutes.
RUBY: We used to carry a lighting rig up three flights of stairs.
NIA: We were strong.
RUBY: We were exhausted.
**ACTION:** Both pause.
NIA: I don't want to become a manager who never makes anything.
RUBY: And I don't want us to lose our friendship trying to build a company.
NIA: Those are two different fears.
RUBY: Then let's be brave about both.

### SCENE 4 — THE TEAM CONVERSATION
**SHOT:** The full group around the big table. No dramatic speeches; simple, respectful conversation.
NIA: The pilot is finished. There is no automatic full-time job here. We'd like to know whether you want to collaborate again.
JULES: Provided nobody orders a table without a measuring tape.
IMANI: Provided we write the scope before the enthusiasm arrives.
VAL: I want a set where I can actually see the light.
SAGE: And I want to pick a background before somebody paints over it.
MATEO: And a writing deadline before the final export.
NOVA: And one shared file naming system. One.
JUNE: I liked that we listened to real people.
SIMONE: I liked that the real people remembered us.
RUBY: I liked all of it. Even when you drove me insane.
NIA: Especially when they drove you insane.
**ACTION:** Laughter breaks the tension.
IMANI: We're all freelancers with other commitments. A repeatable roster and realistic lead time would make sense.
NIA: That's exactly what I was hoping you'd say.

### SCENE 5 — MAKE IT PERSONAL
**SHOT:** June interviews each teammate for a behind-the-scenes closing piece. Brief intercut portrait setups.
JUNE: One word for the season.
MATEO: Loud.
NOVA: Render.
SAGE: Almost.
VAL: Light.
SIMONE: Connections.
JULES: Pivot.
IMANI: Scope.
RUBY: Chaos.
NIA: Trust.
JUNE: Interesting. You didn't say spreadsheet.
NIA: I considered it.
**ACTION:** June sets down her microphone.
RUBY: And you?
JUNE: Seen.
**ACTION:** A quiet beat. Ruby squeezes June's shoulder.
MATEO (O.S.): I preferred "loud." More accurate.
**ACTION:** Everyone laughs again.

### SCENE 6 — THE GROUP PHOTO MISSION
**SHOT:** Val sets timer and tripod. Sage straightens the backdrop. Nova checks focus peaking.
VAL: Everyone inside the tape marks. No improvising.
SAGE: I need the front row to move an inch.
MATEO: My ankles are unionized.
JULES: Can we do one photo without choreography?
SIMONE: Wait. My hair.
IMANI: Wait. Somebody's blocking the exit.
RUBY: I can stand on a chair.
NIA: No chairs. We learned from the launch.
**ACTION:** Three failed takes in rapid succession: someone blinks, the lens cap is on, Ruby is outside frame.
VAL: Why am I always the only person not in the photo?
JUNE: Your job deserves to be in it too.
**ACTION:** June takes the remote and places Val in the group.
JUNE: Everybody just look at each other for one second.
**ACTION:** They do. Genuine laughter. CLICK.

### SCENE 7 — THE PHOTO THEY KEEP
**SHOT:** The group gathers around the camera display.
SAGE: The background is not quite straight.
NOVA: One eye is almost closed.
MATEO: I look like I'm wondering if we got paid.
IMANI: We did.
RUBY: But we look like us.
NIA: We do.
**ACTION:** Nia chooses the imperfect candid shot as the keeper.
NIA: Put this on the studio wall.
VAL: Not the best technical photo.
JUNE: The best story.
NIA: All right, team. If you're in, I want to do the next one better.
RUBY: Bigger ideas. Better process. Same chaos.
IMANI: Smaller chaos.
MATEO: Let's negotiate the size.

### SCENE 8 — TAG: ANOTHER CITY?
**SHOT:** The new group portrait hangs beside Nia and Ruby's old two-person photo. Sunset light warms both.
**SFX:** A message alert. Nia opens a new note from Arden.
ARDEN (VOICE MESSAGE): Northlight wants to take the campaign to another city. We'll have a location, travel budget and dates to discuss. Interested?
RUBY: Another city.
NIA: With the whole team?
JULES: I can make a travel call sheet.
SAGE: I need to know the light.
VAL: I need to know the weather.
NOVA: I need to know the storage plan.
MATEO: I need to know if the table is coming.
**ACTION:** Everyone turns toward the enormous table.
RUBY: We can take it apart this time.
IMANI: We are absolutely not taking the table.
NIA: Okay. First decision as a team: leave the table.
**ACTION:** The rubber duck squeaks. Freeze on the candid group photo.
**END OF SEASON TWO.**
