# SEASON 02 · E03 — THE MISSING PRODUCT
**Series:** OFF BRIEF — THE CREATIVE HOUSE
**Setting:** Studio, shipping entrance, storage closet
**Featured cast:** JULES, VAL, NOVA, MATEO, NIA, RUBY (brief)
**Target length:** 6–8 minutes with physical comedy, search montage and reaction holds
**Status:** Full dialogue draft v1 · eight scenes · production not started

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: EMPTY
**SHOT:** Point of view from inside a tall shipping box. Jules opens the flaps. Mateo and Nova appear above her.
JULES: It's here!
**ACTION:** All three look at the completely empty interior.
MATEO: The box is here.
NOVA: Congratulations. We've received a container for disappointment.
JULES: That is not what the tracking page said.
**ACTION:** An absurdly tiny scrap of packing paper floats down from the flap.
NOVA: Do we photograph the paper?
JULES: Give me thirty seconds.
MATEO: The paper has better availability than the product.
**CUT TO:** OFF BRIEF title with the sound of packing tape ripping.

### SCENE 2 — THE MORNING PLAN
**SHOT:** Earlier in the morning. Jules reviews printed call sheets in the studio.
JULES: Today's product is Northlight's modular desk organizer. Hero shot, close-ups, one lifestyle setup, and a fifteen-second ad.
VAL: My lights are up. The set is locked.
NOVA: I cleared the edit schedule. Please don't give me six camera formats again.
RUBY: I gave you one format.
NOVA: At six frame rates.
NIA: Product arrives at ten. Shoot starts ten thirty.
MATEO: And my script begins: "Every little thing has its place."
**ACTION:** A box of stray cables spills on the desk.
NOVA: Except everything in this studio.
JULES: I've got this. Tracking says out for delivery.
NIA: Great. What could go wrong?
**ACTION:** Everyone looks at her.
NIA: Why did I say that out loud?

### SCENE 3 — THE ARRIVAL
**SHOT:** Jules signs for one large box. Mateo holds the door; Val follows with a light meter.
JULES: Wonderful. Thank you!
MATEO: Is the box light, or are you much stronger than you look?
JULES: Both.
**ACTION:** Jules puts it on the hero table; everyone gathers.
VAL: Let's see the finish.
NOVA: Please let it be the correct color.
JULES: Three... two... one!
**ACTION:** Back to the opening shot. Empty.
JULES: That is a sophisticated packaging strategy.
NOVA: You cannot photograph absence.
MATEO: Concept: "Every little thing has escaped."
JULES: Nobody panic.
NIA: I'm not panicking. I'm opening the vendor email.
**ACTION:** Nia's eyebrow twitches.
RUBY: That's her panic face.

### SCENE 4 — THE INVESTIGATION
**SHOT:** Search montage: under chairs, between prop shelves, shipping labels, online manifest.
JULES: The label lists two packages. We got box two of two.
NIA: Where's one of two?
MATEO: Presumably enjoying a private vacation.
NOVA: Is there a delivery photo?
JULES: Yes. A photo of the front door.
VAL: With a box?
JULES: With a thumb over the lens.
RUBY: That's experimental photography.
VAL: It's a war crime against framing.
NIA: We don't call the client with guesses. Jules, contact the courier. Imani will verify the procurement reference.
JULES: On it. And I want a backup schedule.
NOVA: Music to my ears. Literally, I can put it under the montage.

### SCENE 5 — A USEFUL PLAN B
**SHOT:** Val sets three small unbranded cardboard shapes where the product should be. Nova watches monitor playback.
VAL: We can shoot empty backgrounds, lighting tests, hands, and reaction inserts without the product.
NOVA: Those will help me edit as soon as the real hero shot arrives.
MATEO: I can record guide voiceover without making claims.
RUBY: We could turn the missing box into a behind-the-scenes joke.
NIA: For our own channel, maybe. Not for the paid product ad unless they approve.
JULES: Good. This is still a working shoot, not a disaster documentary.
VAL: Could you move the empty box?
MATEO: It's the only talent who showed up on time.
**ACTION:** Val gently kicks it out of frame.
NOVA: That was the best performance all morning.

### SCENE 6 — THE REVEAL
**SHOT:** Jules receives a courier message. She and Nia walk toward storage while everyone waits.
JULES: Package one was delivered at eight. Signed by... "R. Bee."
RUBY: That sounds suspiciously like me.
NIA: Ruby, did you sign for an earlier box?
RUBY: A very handsome box came early. I thought it was my new props.
JULES: Where is it?
RUBY: I put it somewhere safe.
MATEO: Words people say before a tragedy.
**ACTION:** They open the storage closet. A large sealed box sits behind twelve rubber ducks.
NOVA: The ducks were guarding it.
RUBY: They're unionized.
NIA: Please tell me you didn't open the product.
RUBY: Absolutely not.
**ACTION:** She reveals a box cutter in her pocket. Everyone stares.
RUBY: I thought about it artistically.

### SCENE 7 — THE ACTUAL SHOOT
**SHOT:** Proper product: a fictional modular desktop organizer in muted powder blue. Val's camera moves across it. Hands place pens and notebooks into compartments.
VAL: Gorgeous. That's the texture we needed.
NOVA: Cut one works. Can I get two more seconds of the lid opening?
JULES: We still have forty minutes.
MATEO: The line now actually makes sense.
RUBY: And for our behind-the-scenes channel, we have a box mystery.
NIA: Only after we ask everyone if they want to be in it.
NOVA: I'd like my close-up deleted.
VAL: It was mostly your eyebrow.
NOVA: That's my best feature.
JULES: We lost one hour and still finished the list.
NIA: That's why you're here.
JULES: I might put that on my emergency kit.

### SCENE 8 — TAG: DELIVERY FOR WHOM?
**SHOT:** End of day. A much smaller parcel appears at the entrance.
MATEO: Jules? There's another one.
JULES: I am not opening a third package today.
NOVA: It says "For the creative genius."
RUBY: Oh, that's me.
NIA: It could be anyone.
RUBY: That's emotionally devastating.
**ACTION:** Ruby opens it. Inside is a tiny bubble-wrapped rubber duck.
MATEO: Another qualified team member.
NOVA: At least this one fits on the table.
**END — CUT TO CREDITS.**
