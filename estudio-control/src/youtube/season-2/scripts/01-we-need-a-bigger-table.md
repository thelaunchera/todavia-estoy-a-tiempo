# SEASON 02 · E01 — WE NEED A BIGGER TABLE
**Series:** OFF BRIEF — THE CREATIVE HOUSE
**Setting:** Existing creative studio, delivery entrance, shared workspace
**Featured cast:** NIA, RUBY, IMANI, JULES, MATEO (cameo)
**Target length:** 6–8 minutes including reaction holds and visual montage
**Status:** Full dialogue draft v1 · eight scenes · production not started

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE TABLE
**SHOT:** Locked-off low camera, inside the studio facing its narrow entrance. A huge worktable slowly enters frame. We see only the legs of people pushing it and the table edge wedged in the doorway.
**SFX:** Wood scraping. Shoe squeaks. A heroic effort grunted by off-screen humans.
NIA: Who measured the door?
RUBY (O.S.): You said we needed room to grow.
NIA: I meant professionally.
JULES (O.S.): One second! I'm rotating it!
**ACTION:** The table moves two centimeters, then stops. A rubber duck on the shelf falls over.
IMANI (O.S.): Does anyone have the receipt?
RUBY: The receipt doesn't fit through the door either.
**ACTION:** Nia slowly stares into camera. Sharp title sting: OFF BRIEF.

### SCENE 2 — TWO DAYS EARLIER: THE YES
**SHOT:** Familiar studio at morning light. A laptop pings; Nia almost drops her coffee.
NIA: Ruby. Sit down.
RUBY: I don't like that opening.
NIA: Northlight approved the paid pilot.
RUBY: I love that opening. Say it again slower.
NIA: Paid. Pilot.
RUBY: Are we rich?
NIA: We are employed for a limited period, with a defined budget.
RUBY: So we're emotionally rich.
NIA: They need a launch film, six cutdowns, behind-the-scenes, and photography.
RUBY: We can do that.
NIA: Two people cannot do that in twelve days and sleep.
RUBY: You're always introducing sleep into creative decisions.
NIA: Because I remember what happened last season.
**ACTION:** Ruby glances at the patched tripod. It squeaks.
RUBY: Fine. We hire help.
NIA: Freelancers. Paid roles. Clear contracts.
RUBY: A creative house.
NIA: A temporary production team.
RUBY: You're making my words sad.
NIA: I'm making our bank account survive.

### SCENE 3 — THE INTERVIEWS
**SHOT:** Nia's desk becomes a tiny interview station. A sign with handwritten blank space waits for typography.
JULES: I coordinated a five-location shoot during a rainstorm. I can handle this.
RUBY: Have you ever handled a printer that refuses to print?
JULES: With dignity? No. With tape? Yes.
NIA: Jules, what do you need from us?
JULES: One decision-maker, one actual schedule, and please don't move the shoot by text message at midnight.
RUBY: That last one seems personally targeted.

**ACTION:** Jules exits; Imani enters with a slim binder.
IMANI: Before I accept, I need the scope, actual client budget, payment milestones, and the list of things you've already promised.
NIA: Finally. My language.
RUBY: Are you two going to start a spreadsheet together?
IMANI: Only if she labels the tabs.
NIA: I label my tabs.
RUBY: Oh no. There are two of them.
IMANI: We also need a contingency line.
RUBY: For what?
IMANI: Whatever you're about to say next.

### SCENE 4 — THE TEAM PLAN
**SHOT:** Nia adds names to a whiteboard. Ruby adds colorful shapes to the same board. Jules holds a clipboard; Imani opens a calculator.
NIA: Val handles camera. Nova edits. Mateo writes. Jules runs the schedule. Imani owns production approvals.
RUBY: And I direct.
IMANI: Direct creative decisions, yes. Not purchase orders.
RUBY: That's the cruelest sentence I've heard today.
JULES: What's the call time?
RUBY: Sunrise-ish.
JULES: Sunrise is a natural event, not a call time.
NIA: Eight thirty.
RUBY: Sunrise with boundaries.
IMANI: Excellent. One more thing. Where is everyone working?
**ACTION:** The three women look at the tiny folding desk and two chairs.
RUBY: We make space.
NIA: We rent a table.
JULES: I know a secondhand furniture warehouse.
IMANI: Has anyone measured the door?
**ACTION:** Silence.
RUBY: That feels like a very specific question.

### SCENE 5 — THE DELIVERY
**SHOT:** A giant flat-pack table arrives. Jules energetically directs three people, Nia measures interior floor, Ruby sets up a dramatic entrance playlist.
JULES: Okay, wide side first. Pivot on my count.
NIA: Did we check the exterior width?
JULES: The listing said compact.
IMANI: The listing said compact *for a conference room*.
RUBY: On the positive side, the table is gorgeous.
NIA: It's blocking our business.
RUBY: Gorgeous businesses face obstacles.
**ACTION:** The table tilts slightly. A coffee mug nearly slides off a shelf. Nia catches it.
NIA: Do not touch the light stand!
JULES: I wasn't going to!
**ACTION:** Jules does touch it. Ruby catches the stand. Everyone freezes.
IMANI: I am adding a safety briefing to the schedule.
RUBY: The table has already brought us closer.

### SCENE 6 — BACK TO THE COLD OPEN
**SHOT:** Return to the exact static doorway POV from Scene 1.
NIA: Who measured the door?
RUBY: You said we needed room to grow.
IMANI: Everyone stop pushing.
**ACTION:** The room becomes very quiet. Imani looks at the table legs.
IMANI: Those legs come off.
JULES: I knew that.
IMANI: Why did you leave them on?
JULES: Confidence?
RUBY: It's a growth mindset.
NIA: It's a furniture mindset.
**ACTION:** Mateo appears on the other side of the door with a notebook.
MATEO: Are you opening a studio or building a barricade?
NIA: We're taking the legs off.
MATEO: Bold strategy. Doors love that.
**ACTION:** Cut to a fast, satisfying montage of unscrewing legs, carrying the top through, and rebuilding. The table fits.

### SCENE 7 — FIRST TEAM MEETING
**SHOT:** The newly assembled table fills much of the cramped studio. Five seats; a half-built lighting rig in the background.
NIA: Welcome. The client is buying a defined pilot, not every idea we think of.
RUBY: But we will have excellent ideas.
IMANI: Which we will price before building.
JULES: And schedule before promising.
MATEO: Do I need to write that on the wall?
NIA: Actually, yes.
RUBY: First official creative-house rule: nobody works all night just because we were too excited to plan.
NIA: That's... a surprisingly good rule.
IMANI: Second rule: one person owns each decision.
JULES: Third rule: shoes off the table.
**ACTION:** All look at Ruby, who has one sneaker on the table trying to see a camera angle.
RUBY: Fine. You people are very demanding.

### SCENE 8 — TAG: THE TABLE'S NEW JOB
**SHOT:** Studio after hours. Mateo quietly writes copy at the table; Nia opens her laptop.
MATEO: Is this table always this large?
NIA: We're expanding.
MATEO: It's currently expanding my rent anxiety.
RUBY (O.S.): Everybody, good news! I bought matching chairs.
NIA: How many?
RUBY (O.S.): Twelve.
**ACTION:** Nia looks at the doorway again.
IMANI (O.S.): Are they assembled?
RUBY (O.S.): ...Define assembled.
**END — CUT TO CREDITS.**
