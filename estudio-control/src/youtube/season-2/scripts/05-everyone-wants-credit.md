# SEASON 02 · E05 — EVERYONE WANTS CREDIT
**Series:** OFF BRIEF — THE CREATIVE HOUSE
**Setting:** Editing bay, creative studio, remote client approval call
**Featured cast:** NIA, RUBY, NOVA, MATEO, VAL, SAGE, IMANI, JULES, SIMONE, JUNE
**Target length:** 6–8 minutes including extended scrolling-credit gag
**Status:** Full dialogue draft v1 · eight scenes · production not started

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: ENDLESS CREDITS
**SHOT:** Total black. Elegant white credits crawl upward over soaring orchestral music; one list of names after another.
**ACTION:** Fifteen seconds pass. Another page of credits. A tiny rubber duck gets an assistant producer credit.
NIA (O.S.): Why are the credits longer than the commercial?
MATEO (O.S.): Everyone wanted recognition.
NOVA (O.S.): The ad is fifteen seconds. Credits are three minutes.
RUBY (O.S.): But they're beautifully typeset.
SAGE (O.S.): Thank you.
**ACTION:** A line for "Coffee machine — emotional support" scrolls past.
IMANI (O.S.): Is the coffee machine billing us?
**CUT TO:** OFF BRIEF logo and a record scratch.

### SCENE 2 — THE FIRST ASSEMBLY
**SHOT:** Editing bay. Nova plays a polished fifteen-second cut of the Northlight spot while the team squeezes around one screen.
NOVA: Picture lock candidate. Product reads, story is clean, sound is almost final.
VAL: The push-in works.
SAGE: The colors finally match the brand.
MATEO: My line survived.
RUBY: It feels like us.
NIA: Great. We can send to the client.
SIMONE: One quick thing. Where are our credits?
NOVA: In the end card metadata.
SIMONE: Not in the video?
JUNE: The client only approved a short ad.
JULES: There are ten of us. You want ten names in fifteen seconds?
SIMONE: I want people to know who made it.
IMANI: Reasonable question. Not necessarily a reasonable frame count.
MATEO: We can put the names in tiny letters.
NOVA: If they need a microscope, that isn't recognition.

### SCENE 3 — A CREDIT FOR EVERYTHING
**SHOT:** A huge chaotic paper list of contributions rapidly expands across the studio wall.
VAL: Director of photography. That's straightforward.
SAGE: Color design and set styling. Two distinct jobs.
MATEO: Writer. Concept collaborator. Emergency prop therapist.
RUBY: I don't remember approving that last one.
MATEO: I helped the coffee cup with its emotional arc.
JULES: Coordination. Permits. Deliveries. The table.
IMANI: The table is not a person.
JULES: It has taken up more of my life than most people.
JUNE: Can we recognize the bookstore owner and crew who agreed to share their work?
NIA: Yes. With permission and correct names.
SIMONE: What about people who came in for two hours?
NOVA: Also deserve accurate credit, just not an unreadable screen.
RUBY: Let's not solve an honest issue with a thousand-point font.
**ACTION:** Ruby places a rubber duck at the bottom of the list.
IMANI: The duck is not a production assistant.
JULES: It helped morale.
NOVA: I'm starting to feel underpaid by the duck.

### SCENE 4 — THE BAD TEST
**SHOT:** Nova plays Version B, a credit-heavy mock-up.
NOVA: Okay. Here's a version with everything on screen.
**ACTION:** Names fly past in a blur. The product is tiny in the top-left corner.
SAGE: It's like reading the terms and conditions during a car chase.
VAL: You can't even see the shot.
MATEO: My name is gone before my mother could pronounce it.
SIMONE: I did ask for recognition, not motion sickness.
NIA: We're not using this.
RUBY: Wait. Pause at the duck.
**ACTION:** The duck credit appears with a gold star.
IMANI: Who gave it a star?
JULES: Team morale.
IMANI: You're all going to give me a star-shaped headache.

### SCENE 5 — A REAL CONVERSATION
**SHOT:** The exhausted team sits around the oversized table. Nia speaks more quietly.
NIA: I think we're arguing because some of you don't know whether anyone notices your work.
**ACTION:** Nobody jokes.
JUNE: People notice the finished image. They don't see the person who got consent or kept the subject comfortable.
VAL: Or the twenty light changes.
NOVA: Or a cut I spent four hours fixing.
SAGE: Or the colors that are right precisely because nobody notices them.
IMANI: And if this grows, we need transparent credits in contracts, not a scramble at export.
RUBY: You're right. Creative direction doesn't mean taking credit for every good idea.
MATEO: In that case, I would like to withdraw my rubber-duck therapy title.
JULES: It was your best work.
NIA: Let's document contributions properly and ask the client for an approved place to publish them.

### SCENE 6 — THE CREDIT SYSTEM
**SHOT:** Collaborative spreadsheet and a clean behind-the-scenes page mock-up. No identifiable personal data.
IMANI: We list the agreed role, who performed it, and whether they approve public listing.
JULES: I can confirm everyone who worked on set.
JUNE: I can ask our interview participant how she wants to be named.
NOVA: I can keep the actual ad intact.
SIMONE: And I can make a separate post celebrating the team with permission.
RUBY: Plus a short making-of, where the invisible work becomes visible.
SAGE: Can it be designed beautifully?
NIA: Yes.
SAGE: Then I agree.
MATEO: Write that down. We achieved peace through typography.

### SCENE 7 — THE CLIENT APPROVES
**SHOT:** Nia and Ruby on a video call with Arden; the rest of the team listens from just outside frame.
ARDEN: The launch film is great. We can credit your studio in the campaign description and link to a behind-the-scenes piece.
NIA: Perfect. We'll confirm any individual names before publishing.
ARDEN: Appreciate the care.
RUBY: Our crew made this better than either of us could alone.
**ACTION:** The team exchange pleased looks, then try not to look too pleased.
NOVA: Is that a compliment?
VAL: Shh. Don't scare it.
ARDEN: We also have a launch event date. Are you ready?
**ACTION:** Everyone turns toward Jules.
JULES: Technically, yes. Emotionally, let's revisit.

### SCENE 8 — TAG: ONE FINAL CREDIT
**SHOT:** Studio screen displays tasteful, readable credits on a separate behind-the-scenes page.
NOVA: Finished. Everyone approved the names.
RUBY: Great. And the duck?
IMANI: No.
**ACTION:** A small squeak interrupts. Camera pans to the duck with a paper badge.
MATEO: Don't be jealous. It negotiated no overtime.
NIA: What does its badge say?
JULES: "Union Representative."
**ACTION:** Imani slowly removes the badge.
IMANI: Meeting adjourned.
**END — CUT TO CREDITS.**
