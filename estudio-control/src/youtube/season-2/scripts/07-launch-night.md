# SEASON 02 · E07 — LAUNCH NIGHT
**Series:** OFF BRIEF — THE CREATIVE HOUSE
**Setting:** Small polished event venue, backstage area, projection booth
**Featured cast:** NIA, RUBY, SIMONE, VAL, NOVA, JULES, IMANI, SAGE, MATEO, JUNE, ARDEN
**Target length:** 6–8 minutes including countdown, staged launch and audience reaction holds
**Status:** Full dialogue draft v1 · eight scenes · production not started

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: ZERO
**SHOT:** Giant LED countdown at a launch venue: three, two, one. Confetti explodes. Guests clap. The main projection screen remains perfectly black.
**SHOT:** Extreme close-up of Nia's controlled smile beginning to wobble.
NIA: Tell me that's part of the show.
NOVA (IN HEADSET): That is not part of the show.
SIMONE (ONSTAGE, TO AUDIENCE): And now... a moment of dramatic suspense.
JULES (IN HEADSET): It's been eleven seconds.
RUBY: Suspense is getting expensive.
**ACTION:** One lonely rubber duck squeak comes from the sound system.
**CUT TO:** OFF BRIEF title over a cut-off party horn.

### SCENE 2 — MORNING OF THE LAUNCH
**SHOT:** Venue at sunrise. Crew installs lights, props, a screen and a small welcome table.
JULES: Doors at seven. Test at five. Talent call at five thirty.
IMANI: We have insurance, safety clearance, and vendor confirmations.
SIMONE: I have forty-two guests confirmed and one journalist who says they're "very curious."
MATEO: That's either excellent or terrifying.
VAL: Camera positions are mapped. No one steps into the lens line during the reveal.
RUBY: We're about to have a real launch.
NIA: We have to deliver it first.
JUNE: Can I get a couple of quiet interviews with the team before it starts?
NIA: Ask individually. If someone says no, no problem.
NOVA: No interview for me until the backup screen is checked.
RUBY: Wow. Romantic. A woman who loves redundancy.
NOVA: I love the kind of redundancy that prevents national embarrassment.

### SCENE 3 — THE REHEARSAL
**SHOT:** Wide stage. A test film plays smoothly on the main screen.
NOVA: Playback A looks good. B is ready on a local drive.
VAL: I've got independent event coverage. No need for the screen in my shot.
SAGE: The backdrop is one shade warmer than the reference.
IMANI: Is it visible to a normal human?
SAGE: I'm not taking questions at this time.
SIMONE: Two guests have asked if Nia and Ruby are a comedy duo.
RUBY: We are.
NIA: We're not.
RUBY: See? Perfect timing.
JULES: Can we have a rehearsal without stand-up?
MATEO: Absolutely. Our jokes have never been standing.

### SCENE 4 — THE UNEXPECTED GUEST
**SHOT:** Simone meets a guest at the entrance: Mrs. Vell from the first season's candle brand, carrying a gift.
SIMONE: Welcome! I didn't realize you were coming.
MRS. VELL: Nia and Ruby were my first favorite tiny agency.
SIMONE: We prefer "boutique studio."
MRS. VELL: You prefer a lot of things.
**ACTION:** Mrs. Vell gives them a tasteful candle.
RUBY: You came!
MRS. VELL: I saw your invitation. Someone has to support the people who made beige interesting.
NIA: That is the nicest review we have ever received.
**ACTION:** June quietly photographs the moment with permission.
JUNE: That should be in the behind-the-scenes piece.
NIA: After we survive tonight.
MRS. VELL: You look very nervous.
RUBY: That's our premium glow.

### SCENE 5 — THE GLITCH
**SHOT:** Behind the booth, Nova checks the primary projector and backup feed.
NOVA: I don't like that intermittent handshake signal.
JULES: What's the impact?
NOVA: Probably nothing. But I want the backup physically tested one more time.
IMANI: We have time before doors?
JULES: Four minutes.
RUBY: Four beautiful minutes.
**ACTION:** A stagehand unplugs the wrong cable while rearranging a stand. The test screen blinks.
NOVA: Okay. Nobody touches anything.
VAL: I can keep filming regardless.
NOVA: The playback is local, sound is independent, backup is ready. Just don't move the cables.
**ACTION:** Jules marks the floor with brightly colored tape. The same stagehand nods, then almost trips over a coil.
JULES: If that coil were any more dangerous, it would have its own speaking role.

### SCENE 6 — THE COUNTDOWN RETURNS
**SHOT:** Match to cold open. The timer ends. Confetti. The screen remains black.
NOVA (IN HEADSET): Primary output dropped. Switching to B.
IMANI: Confirm before we announce.
SIMONE: Crowd is with us. I can stretch thirty seconds.
RUBY: Keep it warm. No fake story.
NIA: We own the delay. Jules, update Arden. Val, keep filming guests, not the broken screen.
JULES: On it.
NOVA: Backup isn't getting the room feed either. Give me the side monitor.
**ACTION:** Ruby watches Simone fill the silence with an improvised question.
SIMONE (ONSTAGE): Quick show of hands. Who has ever needed a little help finding their keys in the morning?
**ACTION:** Nearly every guest raises a hand.
RUBY: That's actually our campaign.
NIA: Then we can start without the video.

### SCENE 7 — THE HUMAN LAUNCH
**SHOT:** Ruby and Nia step onstage together. The screen remains dark, but the audience is engaged.
NIA (ONSTAGE): We had a beautiful film ready. The screen has another opinion.
**ACTION:** Audience laughs.
RUBY (ONSTAGE): The campaign is about small things that make a big day easier. So here's the small thing nobody expected: we're going to show you the actual people who made it.
**ACTION:** On cue, Val's camera feed arrives on the side monitors; Jules and Nova restore the backup projection moments later.
NOVA (IN HEADSET): Feed B restored. Go when ready.
NIA (ONSTAGE): And now, here's the film.
**ACTION:** The real campaign launches, followed by sincere applause.
ARDEN (AFTERWARD): That was not what we planned.
NIA: I know. I'm sorry for the delay.
ARDEN: The way you handled it? That was the part I remembered.
RUBY: We specialize in making the unexpected surprisingly billable.
IMANI: We absolutely do not bill for the glitch.
RUBY: Still learning.

### SCENE 8 — TAG: THE AWARD
**SHOT:** After cleanup, the team poses beside a small thank-you table. A rubber duck rests on the projector.
JULES: The launch is done. All equipment accounted for.
NOVA: Backup drives accounted for.
VAL: Memory cards accounted for.
SIMONE: Press mentions accounted for.
IMANI: Invoices accounted for.
NIA: Rubber duck?
MATEO: It carried the show.
**ACTION:** Nova plays one tiny squeak from her phone. Everyone laughs.
JUNE: Hold that. That's the group photo.
**ACTION:** Camera flash just as someone blinks.
