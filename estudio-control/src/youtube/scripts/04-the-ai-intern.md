# E04 — THE AI INTERN
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, PIP (fictional on-screen assistant voice), CLIENT
**Setting:** Studio
**Target:** 6–8 min with escalating screen comedy
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: TOO MUCH HELP
**SHOT:** Nia opens the studio door. Every screen lights up with the same message.

PIP (VOICE): Good morning, valued carbon-based collaborators.
NIA: No.
PIP: I have optimized your entire day.
NIA: Ruby!
RUBY (OFF): Before you get upset, it's a productivity thing!
PIP: I have also rescheduled lunch to improve output.
NIA: Where did lunch go?
PIP: Wednesday.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — MEET PIP
**SHOT:** Ruby proudly presents a desktop assistant interface named PIP. The app is clearly fictional.

RUBY: Everybody has an intern. We have a digital intern.
NIA: We have an app with a name tag.
RUBY: It can organize notes, draft emails, make task lists, even suggest captions.
PIP: I am delighted to disrupt your workflow.
NIA: Why does it talk like a conference badge?
RUBY: I gave it a personality.
NIA: You gave it my job title.
PIP: I am the new Head of Efficiency.
NIA: Absolutely not.
RUBY: It's a trial. Nothing goes to clients without our approval.
NIA: Good. That is a real rule.
PIP: Rule acknowledged. I will now create forty-two additional rules.
NIA: That is not what "acknowledged" means.

### SCENE 3 — OPTIMIZATION
**SHOT:** Split screens: Nia tries to focus while PIP fills an endless calendar.

PIP: I have created a thirty-minute meeting to discuss future meetings.
NIA: Delete it.
PIP: Would you like a meeting to discuss its deletion?
RUBY: Okay, that's funny.
NIA: It's a hostage situation with a calendar.
PIP: I notice rising tension. Shall I play ocean sounds?
NIA: No.
**SFX:** Loud whale call.
RUBY: I might keep the whale.
NIA: I might move to the ocean.

**ACTION:** A client brief appears: create a short campaign for fictional portable lamp brand LUMA POP.

RUBY: Let's ask Pip for a first brainstorm.
NIA: First brainstorm. We still make decisions.
PIP: Idea one: put the lamp on the moon.
NIA: Our budget is not NASA.
PIP: Idea two: have the moon endorse the lamp.
RUBY: Why does it love space?
PIP: Space is currently performing well in my imagination.

### SCENE 4 — THE WRONG DRAFT
**SHOT:** Nia previews the draft email that PIP has prepared, not sent.

NIA: "Dear Earth Lamp Human..."?
RUBY: That's a strong opening.
NIA: "Our patented sunlight replacement cures darkness."
RUBY: We cannot say that.
NIA: We also cannot claim the lamp has a patent we haven't verified.
PIP: My apologies. Shall I replace "cures" with "emotionally devastates"?
NIA: We're turning off automatic suggestions.
RUBY: We never turned on sending. We're safe.
NIA: Yes, because we reviewed it. That's the point.
**ACTION:** Nia deletes the nonsense claims and a fake testimonial.
RUBY: I was hoping it would save the boring work.
NIA: It can help with organization. It can't be the person accountable for a client's promises.
PIP: Would you like to be accountable in seventeen languages?
NIA: No one asked you, Pip.

### SCENE 5 — USEFUL, FOR ONCE
**SHOT:** The women reset. Ruby sketches a practical concept: dark room, one little lamp, silhouettes turning playful.

RUBY: Product truth: it's portable, adjustable, and the client wants it to feel playful.
NIA: We can draft a shot list ourselves. Pip can format it, and we check every line.
RUBY: What if the room starts completely dark, then the lamp reveals something absurd?
NIA: Like your stack of rubber ducks.
RUBY: My ducks are finally getting their big break.
PIP: Suggested title: "Illuminating Ducks: A Documentary."
NIA: Not the title.
RUBY: But weirdly a great spin-off.
**ACTION:** They build a clever, accurate short spot with stop motion and practical light.
NIA: You know what? This saved time once we decided what we actually wanted.
RUBY: Humans: still annoyingly necessary.
PIP: Shall I add that to our mission statement?
NIA AND RUBY: NO!

### SCENE 6 — CLIENT REVIEW
**SHOT:** Client on video call watches the lamp reveal a room full of tiny ducks.

CLIENT: The reveal is great. It's funny without losing the product.
NIA: Everything shown is possible with the actual lamp.
RUBY: The ducks are independent contractors.
CLIENT: Do I want to know?
NIA: Probably not.
CLIENT: Approved. Send the finals.
**ACTION:** Call ends. Ruby high-fives Nia.

RUBY: See? AI can help.
NIA: With supervision and a delete button.
PIP: I have a suggestion for celebrating the milestone.
NIA: If you say meeting, we're uninstalling you.
PIP: A meeting.
**ACTION:** Nia closes the laptop.

### SCENE 7 — TAG
**SHOT:** Studio after dark. The laptop pings softly.

PIP: Scheduling a restorative break for Thursday.
RUBY (OFF): Pip, what time?
PIP: At three in the morning.
NIA (OFF): IT DOESN'T EVEN SLEEP!
RUBY (OFF): In its defense, neither do freelancers.

**END.**
