# E01 — THE VIRAL EMERGENCY
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, CLIENT (voice message)
**Setting:** Studio, early Monday morning
**Target:** 6–8 min with physical comedy and intentional reaction holds
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE FLOOR CAM
**SHOT:** Extreme low angle from a fallen camera. Nia and Ruby lean into frame. Dust floats above the lens. Hold two seconds too long.

NIA: Is it broken?
RUBY: Technically... it's still recording.
NIA: Ruby. Why is the camera on the floor?
RUBY: Creative direction.
NIA: Gravity is not a creative director.
RUBY: Actually, gravity is doing incredible work in this shot.
NIA: Why do I smell burnt coffee?
RUBY: That's... a separate department.
NIA: We have two departments. You and me.
RUBY: Then I have some difficult news for management.

**ACTION:** A paper backdrop collapses off-screen. Both stare toward the noise without moving their heads.

NIA: I'm going to need the entire morning explained.
RUBY: Okay, but you have to promise not to interrupt.
NIA: I promise nothing.

**CUT TO:** OFF BRIEF title card. A tiny rubber duck squeaks over the theme.

### SCENE 2 — THREE HOURS EARLIER
**SHOT:** Symmetrical studio wide. Nia's immaculate workstation versus Ruby's mountains of swatches and props.

NIA: New week. New clients. New boundaries.
RUBY: That sounds like something you wrote on a mug.
NIA: I did. You broke it.
RUBY: The mug had weak boundaries.
NIA: Today we finish the shot list, organize the invoices, and finally replace the tripod.
RUBY: The tripod is fine.
**ACTION:** Behind Ruby, the tripod slowly folds in on itself.
NIA: It's doing yoga without permission.
RUBY: It's a wellness brand now.
NIA: What are those?
RUBY: Miniature traffic cones. I bought twelve.
NIA: We sell content, not traffic safety.
RUBY: You say that like there's no crossover.

**SFX:** Email notification. They turn to the laptop.

### SCENE 3 — THE BRIEF
**SHOT:** Over-the-shoulder on an email from fictional brand MORROW COFFEE.

NIA: We have an inquiry. A real one.
RUBY: From someone who isn't your cousin?
NIA: Please don't make this moment smaller.
RUBY: I'm emotionally standing on a chair.

NIA: "We need a campaign that's minimal, luxurious, colorful, funny, emotional and viral."
RUBY: That's six different meetings in one sentence.
NIA: "Budget: fifty dollars. Delivery: today."
RUBY: Fifty dollars?
NIA: Maybe they forgot a zero.
RUBY: Or three. I like the version where they forgot three.
NIA: It's a small test project. They say there's a larger budget if it works.
RUBY: Every bad date says there's potential.
NIA: We agree to one short concept. No free extras.
RUBY: Look at you. Boundaries on a Monday.
NIA: I don't like the tone.
RUBY: It's my supportive tone. I own one.

**ACTION:** Nia types a short scope confirmation. The client replies with six thumbs-up emojis.

NIA: That seems promising.
RUBY: Those are never legally binding.

### SCENE 4 — CONCEPT LAB
**SHOT:** Rapid montage: Ruby pins cards to the wall; Nia starts a thirty-minute timer.

RUBY: Okay. A coffee cup floating in deep space.
NIA: We don't have a spaceship.
RUBY: We have black paper and ambition.
NIA: Next.
RUBY: A dramatic courtroom. The defendant is a sleepy espresso bean.
NIA: Next.
RUBY: A love story between a woman and her morning latte.
NIA: I'm begging you to remember we have one product and a table.
RUBY: You're killing the arts.
NIA: I'm protecting the invoice.
RUBY: What if the product is the hero, but the world around it is chaos?
NIA: That's our literal business model.
RUBY: Exactly. Authenticity.

**ACTION:** Nia pauses the timer. She likes it.

NIA: Fine. Beautiful product shot. Unexpected chaotic reveal. We can do that.
RUBY: You said "we can do that" without sighing. Write this date down.
NIA: Don't make it weird.
RUBY: Too late. It's in my calendar.

### SCENE 5 — ONE PERFECT SHOT
**SHOT:** Product set. A cinematic macro shot of a coffee mug. Ruby adjusts flags. Nia watches exposure and framing.

NIA: Cup centered. Clean backdrop. Light at forty-five degrees.
RUBY: The cup looks lonely.
NIA: The cup is not sentient.
RUBY: It has a story.
NIA: Its story is hot liquid.
RUBY: That's a sad way to live.
NIA: Okay. We have ten minutes before the light shifts.
RUBY: Can I add atmosphere?
NIA: Define atmosphere.
RUBY: A whisper of smoke.
NIA: How much smoke?
RUBY: A whisper with excellent stage presence.

**ACTION:** Ruby activates a tiny fog machine. A ridiculous cloud pours out.

NIA: That's not a whisper. That's an evacuation.
RUBY: The machine has never met subtlety.
NIA: Turn it off!
RUBY: I am! It's responding emotionally!
**ACTION:** Nia reaches past the light. The unstable tripod tips. Coffee splashes. Camera falls. CRASH.

### SCENE 6 — BACK TO THE FLOOR
**SHOT:** Match-cut to the exact first shot from Scene 1.

NIA: I'm going to need the entire morning explained.
RUBY: So technically, you were there for most of it.
NIA: We destroyed a set for a fifty-dollar job.
RUBY: We still have the footage.
NIA: It's footage of a table having a nervous breakdown.
RUBY: Wait.
**ACTION:** Ruby rotates the camera playback. The fallen lens captured a perfect slow-motion coffee droplet, the cup landing in frame, and their startled reflections.

RUBY: Look at that.
NIA: The coffee splash?
RUBY: The light through it. The reflections. It's accidental cinema.
NIA: The product is actually beautiful.
RUBY: I know.
NIA: You did not know.
RUBY: I knew retroactively.
NIA: Can we cut it before the table falls?
RUBY: We absolutely can.
NIA: Then we have a campaign.
RUBY: Say it like you mean it.
NIA: We. Have. A campaign.
RUBY: I want that engraved on my tombstone.

### SCENE 7 — DELIVERY
**SHOT:** Split screen: Nia edits rhythmic cuts while Ruby chooses a restrained palette. Their styles finally cooperate.

NIA: Thirty seconds. No extra shots. No fake promises.
RUBY: Beautiful opening, weird little accident, perfect product ending.
NIA: It actually tells a story.
RUBY: I occasionally have one of those.
NIA: Don't let it go to your head.
RUBY: Too late. There's a parade.

**ACTION:** The upload bar reaches 100 percent. Both hold their breath. Notification.

CLIENT (VOICE MESSAGE): Oh my gosh, we love it! This feels so real. One tiny change?
NIA: No.
RUBY: We haven't heard the change.
NIA: I heard the words "tiny change." My body knows.
CLIENT (VOICE MESSAGE): Could we make the coffee pink?
**ACTION:** Absolute silence. Nia closes the laptop slowly.

RUBY: Technically, we could.
NIA: Technically, we could also move to the mountains.
RUBY: Do mountains have Wi-Fi?
NIA: We're going home.
RUBY: We work from home tomorrow?
NIA: We live together in an office now. Apparently.

### SCENE 8 — TAG
**SHOT:** The broken tripod stands in the corner, taped together. Nia puts up a sign: DO NOT TOUCH.

RUBY: Great news. I fixed the tripod.
NIA: Why is there a rubber duck on top?
RUBY: Stability. And leadership.
**ACTION:** Tripod collapses again. The duck squeaks.

NIA: We're putting it on the invoice.
RUBY: Under equipment?
NIA: Under emotional damages.

**END.**
