# E08 — WE ALMOST QUIT
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, ARDEN (voice message), MRS. VELL (voice message)
**Setting:** Studio after the Northlight pitch
**Target:** 6–8 min with emotional payoff and comedy
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE EMPTY STUDIO
**SHOT:** Quiet room. A single lamp is on. Nia folds a backdrop. Ruby takes down a mood board. Neither looks at the other.

RUBY: Are we closing?
NIA: We're just cleaning.
RUBY: You never clean the idea wall.
NIA: It was covered in tape.
RUBY: You love tape.
NIA: I love tape that holds things together.
**ACTION:** They look at the blank wall.
RUBY: Oh.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — THE BAD NEWS
**SHOT:** Twenty-four hours earlier. Arden's voice message plays on Nia's phone.

ARDEN (VOICE MESSAGE): We loved your proposal. Leadership is pausing the pilot while budgets are reviewed. We don't have an approval date yet. I'm sorry.
**ACTION:** Nia puts the phone facedown.

RUBY: So that's a no?
NIA: It's a not-now with a very expensive suit.
RUBY: Did they say when?
NIA: No. And our rent is still due when rent is due.
RUBY: We have other clients.
NIA: Two invoices coming in, equipment costs, subscriptions, and a camera with a trust issue.
RUBY: It's not the camera's fault it fell.
NIA: I KNOW.
**ACTION:** Ruby flinches. Nia immediately softens.
NIA: Sorry. I didn't mean to shout at you.
RUBY: It's okay.
NIA: It's not. I'm scared.
RUBY: Me too.

### SCENE 3 — THE FIGHT THEY AVOIDED
**SHOT:** Nia sits in her office chair. Ruby remains standing at the idea wall.

NIA: I thought if we landed Northlight, everything would finally feel safe.
RUBY: I thought if we made work we loved, the money part would eventually follow.
NIA: Sometimes it doesn't.
RUBY: I know.
NIA: Do you? When you say yes to an extra idea, I have to find the extra hours.
RUBY: And when you say no before I've finished explaining, I feel like I'm just a problem you manage.
NIA: You're not a problem.
RUBY: Then stop talking to me like one.
**ACTION:** Silence. The coffee machine makes an unhappy sputter.
NIA: We need real boundaries. Budgets, time, what projects we're willing to take.
RUBY: And space to make work that feels like ours.
NIA: Both.
RUBY: Both.
**ACTION:** Neither is ready to hug. Nia returns to the spreadsheet; Ruby begins removing swatches. The distance feels genuine.

### SCENE 4 — WHAT THEY BUILT
**SHOT:** After hours. Nia finds the broken tripod and a folder of previous campaigns. Ruby finds their first handmade sign.

RUBY: Remember the morning we opened?
NIA: I remember the electrician asking if we knew the building had no outlets on this wall.
RUBY: We said we'd figure it out.
NIA: We had six dollars left for lunch.
RUBY: We bought one sandwich.
NIA: You ate the bigger half.
RUBY: I was carrying the vision.
**ACTION:** They laugh, then quiet down.

RUBY: I don't want a giant agency if it means we stop being friends.
NIA: I don't want to lose the studio because I was too proud to ask for help.
RUBY: Maybe we don't need to become something else to make this work.
NIA: We need to become better at being this.
RUBY: That sounded annoyingly inspirational.
NIA: I learned from the most annoying person I know.
RUBY: Your mom?
NIA: You.
**ACTION:** Ruby places the first handmade studio sign back on the wall.

### SCENE 5 — A NEW PLAN
**SHOT:** Morning. Nia draws three columns on the whiteboard: PAY THE BILLS, MAKE SOMETHING GOOD, SLEEP.

NIA: Here's what we do. Fixed scope before production. Deposit before shoot days. Actual delivery estimates.
RUBY: And we leave room for original experiments instead of pretending every experiment is paid client work.
NIA: A monthly mini-series we own.
RUBY: About what?
NIA: I don't know. Maybe what it's actually like trying to build a creative studio.
RUBY: Two best friends. One creative studio. Zero peace.
NIA: That sounds like a terrible business.
RUBY: It sounds like a great show.
NIA: Who would watch us?
RUBY: People who have ever had a client.
NIA: That's a lot of people.
RUBY: Exactly.
**ACTION:** Nia opens a new folder titled OUR SERIES. Ruby sketches a cartoon version of their studio on the whiteboard.

### SCENE 6 — SMALL CLIENT, BIG RELIEF
**SHOT:** The phone rings. Mrs. Vell's name appears.

MRS. VELL (VOICE MESSAGE): Hello, girls! My sister runs a little bookstore. She saw your candle work. She'd like a holiday campaign. Proper budget, no rush. Can you call her?
RUBY: A real budget. Did you hear that?
NIA: I heard "no rush." I want to frame it.
RUBY: We can say yes, but with the new process.
NIA: We can.
RUBY: And the giant client?
NIA: If they return, we talk. We don't build our lives around an approval that hasn't happened.
RUBY: I like that.
**ACTION:** Nia and Ruby sit side by side and draft a clear response together.

### SCENE 7 — THE PROMISE
**SHOT:** Their studio is still messy, but the gear is organized and the idea wall is back.

NIA: We need a rule for the next year.
RUBY: No clients who say "make it viral" with a fifty-dollar budget?
NIA: Strong candidate.
RUBY: No filming with unstable tripods?
NIA: Extremely strong candidate.
RUBY: No pretending we're okay when we're not?
**ACTION:** Nia looks at Ruby, surprised.
NIA: That one.
RUBY: Deal?
NIA: Deal.
**ACTION:** They shake hands formally. Ruby immediately pulls Nia into a hug.
NIA: This was a handshake.
RUBY: I upgraded the package.
NIA: You didn't quote the upgrade.
RUBY: Friends-and-family discount.

### SCENE 8 — TAG AND SEASON HOOK
**SHOT:** Ground-level camera angle, echoing the very first episode. Nia and Ruby lean over a brand-new tripod.

NIA: Is it level?
RUBY: Perfect.
NIA: Are we recording?
RUBY: Yep.
NIA: What's our opening line?
RUBY: "We make content. Chaos comes free."
NIA: Fine. But the chaos needs an invoice.
**ACTION:** Their new email chimes. Subject: "NORTHLIGHT — PILOT APPROVED?" They exchange stunned looks. Freeze frame before opening.

NIA: Do not touch that until we finish this take.
RUBY: I think we might have a second season.

**END OF SEASON ONE.**
