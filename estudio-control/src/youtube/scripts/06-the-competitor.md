# E06 — THE COMPETITOR
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, BECK (fictional rival creative director), MRS. VELL (brief cameo)
**Setting:** Studio, creative networking event
**Target:** 6–8 min with visual comedy and character stakes
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: FAMILIAR RESULTS
**SHOT:** At a creative event, Ruby stares at a poster. Nia approaches with tiny hors d'oeuvres.

RUBY: You know when somebody wears the exact same outfit as you?
NIA: Sadly, yes.
RUBY: What if they also wore your house, your chair, and your personality?
NIA: That would be criminal.
RUBY: Great. Look at that poster.
**ACTION:** The poster features a candle campaign with nearly the same composition as their earlier concept.
NIA: That's not ideal.
RUBY: That's the understatement of the century.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — MEET ALMOST ORIGINAL
**SHOT:** The rival booth is sleek, glossy and aggressively beige. Creative director Beck beams.

BECK: I'm Beck, founder of Almost Original. We create what's next.
RUBY: By looking at what happened last week?
NIA: Ruby.
BECK: Have we met?
RUBY: Spiritually, yes. In our mood boards.
NIA: We run Fuera de Brief Studio. That's a strikingly familiar layout.
BECK: Trends are a shared language.
RUBY: Fascinating. Does shared language include identical punctuation?
**ACTION:** Ruby points to a typo reproduced exactly from one of their rejected sample drafts.
BECK: We have to network. Excuse me.
**ACTION:** Beck flees with an impossibly small appetizer.
NIA: You cannot interrogate someone with canapé evidence.
RUBY: It was a very incriminating canapé.

### SCENE 3 — RECEIPTS
**SHOT:** Back in the studio, Nia opens project files with timestamps and original working drafts.

NIA: We have source files, emails, dates, and concept development. Good.
RUBY: We should post the whole thing online. Let the internet do its thing.
NIA: Not until we know what happened. Public accusations can turn ugly fast.
RUBY: So we're being boring adults again?
NIA: We're being careful adults with folders.
RUBY: The most dangerous kind.
NIA: Maybe it's coincidence. The image has similarities, but the typo makes me concerned.
RUBY: I did not spend six hours perfecting that typo to be robbed.
NIA: We can contact them professionally. And we can stop posting unfinished concepts publicly.
RUBY: Are you telling me my behind-the-scenes feed is a security breach?
NIA: I'm telling you drafts should stay drafts until we're ready.

### SCENE 4 — SOMETHING MORE IMPORTANT
**SHOT:** Nia writes a polite inquiry. Ruby stares at her rejected mood boards.

RUBY: You know what scares me?
NIA: Your shopping cart?
RUBY: Besides that. What if they're better at being us than we are?
NIA: They're not us.
RUBY: Their office has a fountain. Our coffee machine sounds like a motorcycle.
NIA: A fountain doesn't make work original.
RUBY: It makes hydration original.
NIA: The reason our candle concept worked wasn't the amber light. It was how we understood that client.
RUBY: You listened to her. I pushed her. We found the middle.
NIA: Exactly. They can imitate a picture. They can't copy our relationship with the people who trust us.
RUBY: That was disgustingly inspirational.
NIA: Don't tell anyone.

### SCENE 5 — THE CONVERSATION
**SHOT:** Nia calls Beck. Ruby watches, armed with a notebook and an absurdly large pen.

BECK: Hi. We reviewed your message. A freelance supplier sent us a reference board, and our team didn't check its provenance.
NIA: The composition and draft copy came from materials we can document. We need that example removed while you investigate.
BECK: Understood. We have taken it down and are reviewing the supplier's submission.
RUBY (WHISPER): Ask about the typo.
NIA (MUTED): Stop.
BECK: I'm sorry. I should have checked.
NIA: Thank you for taking it seriously. Please confirm in writing.
**ACTION:** Call ends. Nia exhales.
RUBY: We were calm, collected, and deeply annoying.
NIA: That's called professionalism.
RUBY: I want it on a tote bag.

### SCENE 6 — ORIGINAL VOICES
**SHOT:** Mrs. Vell drops by to pick up the finalized candle campaign prints.

MRS. VELL: I saw that other image online. Oddly similar.
NIA: We addressed it. We're focusing on the campaign we made with you.
MRS. VELL: Good. My daughter showed me your behind-the-scenes video. She loved the two of you.
RUBY: Finally, a focus group with taste.
MRS. VELL: I have a friend at Northlight Market. They're looking for a creative agency.
NIA: Northlight? That's a huge retailer.
MRS. VELL: She's expecting an introduction email from you.
**ACTION:** Mrs. Vell exits. Ruby and Nia stare at the doorway.

RUBY: Did beige just change our lives?
NIA: Beige has excellent connections.
RUBY: I'm taking back everything I said about it.
NIA: Everything?
RUBY: Almost everything.

### SCENE 7 — TAG
**SHOT:** Ruby puts a sign over the studio door: ORIGINAL IDEAS ONLY. Nia reads it.

NIA: Why does the sign say "Orignal"?
RUBY: It's a statement about imperfection.
NIA: You misspelled original.
RUBY: Nobody can steal that exact spelling now.
**ACTION:** Nia takes the marker and silently corrects it.

**END.**
