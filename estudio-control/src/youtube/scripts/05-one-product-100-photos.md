# E05 — ONE PRODUCT, 100 PHOTOS
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, MILES (fictional brand rep)
**Setting:** Studio and prop supply store
**Target:** 6–8 min with escalating montage
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE SAME BOTTLE
**SHOT:** A bright blue reusable bottle sits on a pedestal. Dozens of nearly identical photos flash by.

NIA: Photo ninety-seven.
RUBY: It's too similar to photo eighty-three.
NIA: They are all photos of the same bottle.
RUBY: Yes, but this one looks tired.
NIA: It's stainless steel!
RUBY: Burnout doesn't discriminate.
**ACTION:** Nia gently lowers her forehead onto the desk.
RUBY: Do you need me to take a photo of that? It's very relatable.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — THE REQUEST
**SHOT:** Nia opens an email from fictional brand SIPSTER, which sells one reusable bottle in one color.

NIA: They need a hundred unique product images.
RUBY: One hundred? For how many products?
NIA: One.
RUBY: That's not a shoot. That's a long-term relationship.
NIA: It's for their fall and Thanksgiving launch library. Website, social, emails, ads.
RUBY: Great. A lifestyle, a hero shot, an outdoors shot, a flat lay—
NIA: And ninety-six others.
RUBY: Please let me finish being optimistic.
**ACTION:** The client adds a note: "Every image should feel completely different while keeping the same brand aesthetic."
NIA: They want everything to be unique and identical.
RUBY: A metaphor for the modern internet.

### SCENE 3 — UNLIMITED IDEAS, LIMITED SPACE
**SHOT:** Ruby opens a prop cabinet. A wave of fabric, plastic fruit and mini cones falls out.

RUBY: Blue bottle on blue paper. Blue bottle on an orange cube. Blue bottle on a Friendsgiving dinner table with tiny pumpkins.
NIA: We cannot physically stage a hundred sets today.
RUBY: We could build a tiny planet.
NIA: We are not building a tiny planet.
RUBY: That's what you said before we built the moon for the lamp.
NIA: That was a paper circle.
RUBY: Art is about interpretation.
**ACTION:** Nia marks a whiteboard: HERO, DETAIL, LIFESTYLE, MOTION, SEASONAL.
NIA: Ten purposeful setups. Multiple deliberate crops and applications. No padding with duplicates.
RUBY: You just invented a system while I invented a universe.
NIA: Yours needs a storage unit.
RUBY: Mine has better lighting.
NIA: We can get thirty genuinely distinct assets with the current budget. If they want a hundred original scenes, we quote the actual production time.
RUBY: Is that... a boundary?
NIA: I have been practicing.

### SCENE 4 — THE SHOOT MONTAGE
**SHOT:** Rapid cuts of ten inventive setups including one warm Thanksgiving-inspired harvest table. Ruby rotates the product; Nia records filenames and captures.

RUBY: Set one. Hero. The bottle is mysterious.
NIA: The bottle is a bottle.
RUBY: Let it have range.
NIA: Set two. Cap detail.
RUBY: The cap is the supporting actor.
NIA: Set three. Water droplet texture.
RUBY: The cap has been promoted.
**ACTION:** They pose the bottle on a fake rocky landscape. A fan sends a prop leaf onto the lens.
NIA: The wind has entered the frame.
RUBY: Natural drama.
NIA: We're indoors.
RUBY: Indoor drama.

**ACTION:** Nia tries a stop-motion jump shot. Ruby makes the bottle appear to dance. Both laugh for the first time all afternoon.

NIA: I can't believe that actually worked.
RUBY: The bottle wanted to dance.
NIA: Don't make the bottle sentient again.
RUBY: You'll miss it when it wins an award.

### SCENE 5 — THE CLIENT CHECK-IN
**SHOT:** Miles joins the video call. Nia shares contact sheets with clearly differentiated setups.

MILES: These are fantastic. But our brief said a hundred images.
NIA: I wanted to clarify what counts as an asset. We can deliver crop variations from these setups without calling them a hundred different photographs.
RUBY: We don't want you paying for the same bottle moved two inches.
MILES: That's fair. We actually need images for ten campaign placements, each in a few sizes.
NIA: That is a different—and much clearer—request.
RUBY: And we already have the ten setups.
MILES: So you can deliver the sizes?
NIA: Yes, within the revised scope. We'll label the originals and exports separately.
MILES: You just saved our team a lot of confusion.
RUBY: That's our brand. Confusion reduction with occasional furniture damage.

### SCENE 6 — AN UNEXPECTED MESSAGE
**SHOT:** Studio at dusk. Ruby tapes the new shot list to the wall while Nia checks a paid invoice notification.

NIA: Good news. The client approved the scope and paid the production balance.
RUBY: Amazing. Does that mean we can buy a new tripod?
NIA: Yes.
RUBY: And a tiny planet?
NIA: No.
RUBY: One day, you will respect my infrastructure dreams.
**ACTION:** A glossy envelope slides through the studio mail slot. It advertises a rival agency called ALMOST ORIGINAL.

NIA: "Fresh ideas. Familiar results."
RUBY: That's a weird slogan.
**ACTION:** They turn the card over. An image looks unnervingly like their recent candle concept.
NIA: That looks familiar.
RUBY: Because it is familiar.

### SCENE 7 — TAG
**SHOT:** The blue bottle remains on the set. Nia turns off the lights.

RUBY: Do you think it gets lonely at night?
NIA: We're leaving.
RUBY: I'll put it next to the rubber ducks.
NIA: Absolutely not.
**ACTION:** Cut to the bottle surrounded by rubber ducks with tiny name badges.
RUBY (OFF): It has a whole team now.

**END.**
