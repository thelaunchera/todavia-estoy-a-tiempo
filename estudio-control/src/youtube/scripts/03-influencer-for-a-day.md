# E03 — INFLUENCER FOR A DAY
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, JO (fictional client), delivery rider (one line)
**Setting:** Studio, rented loft, city sidewalk
**Target:** 6–8 min with visual montage
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: THE PERFECT MORNING
**SHOT:** Cinematic close-up: satin robe, perfect breakfast, golden sunlight. Nia looks serene while holding a green smoothie.

NIA (TO CAMERA): Some mornings, I choose peace.
**ACTION:** A smoke detector starts screaming. Ruby ducks into frame holding a toaster.
RUBY: Do we know if a croissant is supposed to be on fire?
NIA: CUT!
**ACTION:** Nia slowly lowers the smoothie.
NIA: Some mornings, peace chooses a different address.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — THE NEW CLIENT
**SHOT:** Studio. A brief from fictional skincare company PEBBLE & DEW fills the screen.

NIA: They want a day-in-the-life video featuring real creators. Natural. Effortless. Relatable.
RUBY: We are real creators.
NIA: Yes.
RUBY: Our lives are full of things.
NIA: Mostly overdue invoices and cables.
RUBY: Relatable.
NIA: They want a creator with a glamorous morning routine. Their talent canceled. They asked if we can step in.
RUBY: I volunteer you.
NIA: Why me?
RUBY: You already have the face of someone who owns matching towels.
NIA: I do own matching towels.
RUBY: Exactly. You're qualified.
NIA: We're making one sponsored concept. We disclose that it's an ad.
RUBY: Amazing. Ethical glamour.
NIA: And you are directing. Not improvising explosives.
RUBY: Those croissants were not explosives. Probably.

### SCENE 3 — THE RENTED LOFT
**SHOT:** Gorgeous loft with white curtains and no working blinds. Ruby arranges props like a magician.

RUBY: Okay, wake up slowly. You're a woman with no bills.
NIA: That's science fiction.
RUBY: Open the curtain like you're opening your soul.
NIA: My soul needs coffee.
RUBY: We have fake coffee. Real coffee stains the cups.
NIA: You rented a kitchen for six hours and banned beverages?
RUBY: Aesthetic discipline.
**ACTION:** Ruby rolls a reflection board into place and blinds Nia.
NIA: My retinas filed a complaint.
RUBY: The light is beautiful.
NIA: The light is a weapon.

**MONTAGE:** Twelve takes of Nia pretending to wake up; Ruby manually tossing flower petals; an expensive-looking breakfast assembled from store-bought pastries.

NIA: How many takes for a person to blink?
RUBY: Seven if she's a professional. Fourteen if she's my best friend.
NIA: I'm billing you for the second seven.

### SCENE 4 — REAL LIFE INVADES
**SHOT:** The doorbell rings repeatedly. Nia is still in a robe and holding a face serum.

DELIVERY RIDER: Package for... "Very Successful Woman"?
RUBY: That's us!
NIA: Why is my name "Very Successful Woman"?
RUBY: It looked good on the delivery label.
**ACTION:** The package contains a replacement ring light, but the screws are missing.
NIA: We have twenty minutes.
RUBY: I can hold the light.
NIA: And the camera?
RUBY: I have two hands.
NIA: And the serum?
RUBY: Right. An unreasonable number of jobs for two arms.
**ACTION:** They try balancing the light on books. Everything wobbles.
NIA: No. We're done pretending.
RUBY: We still owe the client a video.
NIA: Then let's show the truth. The gorgeous shot and what it really took.
RUBY: You want the behind-the-scenes chaos?
NIA: No filters on the chaos. Filters on the actual product shot.
RUBY: That's... annoyingly smart.

### SCENE 5 — A MORE HONEST VIDEO
**SHOT:** Editing montage. Screen alternates between glamorous final shot and chaotic production, both warm and beautifully framed.

RUBY: Opening line: "The routine you saw..."
NIA: "...and the very real people who made it."
RUBY: We keep the product honest. No pretending it cured my stress.
NIA: Also, please stop claiming serums reduce unpaid invoices.
RUBY: They haven't tried it on invoices.
NIA: We don't make claims we can't support.
RUBY: Fine. How about "Looks good in real life, too"?
NIA: That's it.
RUBY: Wait, we're actually good at this.
NIA: That's what I've been trying to tell you since we quit.
RUBY: You mostly say, "Check the calendar."
NIA: That's my love language.

### SCENE 6 — THE CLIENT CALL
**SHOT:** Jo joins by video.

JO: We were expecting something more aspirational.
NIA: The beauty is still there. The second half shows real creators making it happen.
JO: Is that you two trying to hold up a light with a baguette?
RUBY: The baguette had structural potential.
NIA: We've marked all sponsorship disclosures and kept the product demonstration accurate.
JO: My team is laughing. In a good way.
RUBY: We accept laughter as an early payment.
JO: Let's use this. It's memorable, and it feels human.
NIA: Great. We'll send the final export and captions tonight.
**ACTION:** Call ends. Ruby does a tiny victory dance.
RUBY: We became influencers and survived.
NIA: We became women who know where the fire extinguisher is.

### SCENE 7 — TAG
**SHOT:** Back in the real studio. Nia records an unglamorous phone selfie while Ruby eats cereal from a mixing bowl.

NIA (TO CAMERA): My actual morning routine: check the inbox, fix the audio, find a clean cup.
RUBY (OFF): We don't have a clean cup!
NIA: Step four: lie quietly on the floor.
RUBY: That's actually very aspirational.
**ACTION:** Nia turns the camera toward Ruby and they both laugh.

**END.**
