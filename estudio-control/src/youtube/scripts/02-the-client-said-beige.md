# E02 — THE CLIENT SAID BEIGE
**Series:** OFF BRIEF / FUERA DE BRIEF
**Characters:** NIA, RUBY, MRS. VELL (fictional candle brand owner)
**Setting:** Studio and fictional candle shop
**Target:** 6–8 min with reaction beats
**Status:** Full first-draft dialogue · ready for table read

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: BEIGE
**SHOT:** A wall of color swatches fills the frame. Ruby holds a vivid coral chip, Nia a muted cream chip.

RUBY: What feeling does this give you?
NIA: Warmth. Joy. Summer.
RUBY: Great. And this?
NIA: Beige.
RUBY: Exactly. Nothing. The emotional equivalent of a waiting room.
NIA: Our client chose it.
RUBY: Our client chose twenty-three versions of beige.
NIA: There are only twenty-two on the wall.
**ACTION:** Ruby points at Nia's coffee.
RUBY: Twenty-three.
**CUT TO TITLE.**

### SCENE 2 — THE SHOP
**SHOT:** A beautiful but utterly monochromatic candle store. Everything is the same shade.

MRS. VELL: Welcome to Still & Simple. We don't do loud.
RUBY: Your candles are whispering.
MRS. VELL: Exactly. Every picture must feel clean.
NIA: We're proposing a small seasonal launch. What's your ideal customer?
MRS. VELL: Someone calm. Sophisticated. Unbothered.
RUBY: Does this person exist?
NIA: Ruby means we're trying to understand the lifestyle.
MRS. VELL: I trust you two. Just don't make it too creative.
**ACTION:** Ruby chokes on an imaginary sip of water.
RUBY: Sorry. That phrase went down the wrong pipe.
MRS. VELL: Beige. Cream. More beige. Absolutely no red.
NIA: Understood. We'll make you two clear directions.
RUBY (LOW): Is one direction the emergency exit?

### SCENE 3 — A QUIET ARGUMENT
**SHOT:** Back in the studio. Nia builds a mood board. Ruby paces with a box of brightly colored tissue paper.

RUBY: We could make candlelight look like a tiny sunset.
NIA: She literally said no red.
RUBY: She also said "don't make it creative." That's an impossible instruction for a creative company.
NIA: We were hired to listen.
RUBY: Listening isn't disappearing.
NIA: Neither is ignoring a client.
RUBY: Why are we arguing like two divorced parents of a candle?
NIA: Because you keep trying to give the candle a personality.
RUBY: It already has one. It's depressed.
NIA: Listen. We show her what she asked for first. Then offer one bolder option with a reason.
RUBY: A secret fun option?
NIA: An optional concept. With a budget. We are adults.
RUBY: Can we be adults who own pink tissue paper?
NIA: Against my better judgment, yes.

### SCENE 4 — TWO DIRECTIONS
**SHOT:** Side-by-side sets. Left: elegant ivory background, sculptural candle, soft shadows. Right: the same candle with dramatic amber reflection, richly textured props.

NIA: Version A: understated, consistent, premium.
RUBY: Version B: still premium, but it looks like something is happening.
NIA: Please don't pitch it exactly like that.
RUBY: Fine. "A sensory invitation into warmth."
NIA: How are you suddenly so good at client language?
RUBY: I'm bilingual. English and invoice.
**ACTION:** They photograph both. The small studio suddenly feels like a real agency.

### SCENE 5 — THE PRESENTATION
**SHOT:** Mrs. Vell scrolls the first set on a tablet.

MRS. VELL: Beautiful. Very safe.
NIA: It follows the direction you gave us.
MRS. VELL: What's the other one?
RUBY: We explored a second creative concept.
MRS. VELL: I said no red.
RUBY: There is no red. It's amber light. Your actual candle creates it.
NIA: The idea is to keep the clean product identity but give the campaign a recognizable visual signature.
MRS. VELL: That sounds expensive.
NIA: The current scope covers the first option. We can quote the second as an upgrade, or give you the first as agreed.
**ACTION:** Mrs. Vell studies the warm version longer than the beige one.
MRS. VELL: I hate that I like it.
RUBY: That's how most great romances begin.
MRS. VELL: My daughter would share this.
NIA: That's one reason we wanted you to see it.
MRS. VELL: Fine. Quote me the second one.
RUBY: Permission to have feelings?
NIA: Privately.

### SCENE 6 — THE WIN
**SHOT:** Back at the studio. A new invoice goes out. Nia and Ruby dance silently for exactly two seconds before pretending they weren't.

RUBY: You defended my weird idea.
NIA: You delivered something that fit the brand.
RUBY: Are we evolving?
NIA: Don't be dramatic.
RUBY: Our friendship has entered its premium era.
NIA: Our electricity bill has entered its overdue era.
RUBY: Right. Evolution tomorrow.
**ACTION:** Client sends a new email titled "ONE LAST TINY THOUGHT".
NIA: Delete the phrase "tiny thought" from the English language.
RUBY: I'm pretty sure that requires a government form.

### SCENE 7 — TAG
**SHOT:** Ruby quietly replaces Nia's beige sticky notes with color swatches.

NIA: Why is my budget spreadsheet pink?
RUBY: Warm, vibrant, memorable.
NIA: It was a spreadsheet.
RUBY: And now it's a campaign.
NIA: Put the beige back.
**ACTION:** Ruby hands her a swatch labeled "Aggressively Beige."
NIA: You made that up.
RUBY: It's our new color of the year.

**END.**
