Refunds and Purchase Support

Last updated: October 31, 2026

If something is missing or not working

If you've paid but haven't received the PDF, the link fails, a different file arrives or you see a duplicate charge, use the podcast help form. Include the product name, purchase email and what happened. Never send passwords or complete payment-card details.

How the review works

We first review the purchase and delivery to resolve the problem. Refund requests are assessed individually; making a request does not guarantee approval. Mandatory legal rights remain unaffected. If you have questions, contact us before paying.

No recurring subscription for these products

Both digital workbooks are one-time purchases. Subscribing to episode updates is optional and free; you can unsubscribe without affecting your purchase.

Product Center requests

AI assistants, automations, Content Lab and business services may have different contracts, deliveries and billing frequencies. Consult business refunds and subscription cancellation rules for the relevant procedure. Your legal rights remain in place. Commercial support: hello@thelaunchera.com.
