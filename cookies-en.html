Cookies and Browser Storage

Updated: October 31, 2026

Essential storage

The website stores information in your browser as needed for it to function and to remember your privacy choices.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we use a random session identifier to measure song starts and playback progress. Without permission, we do not create that identifier or send playback events. You can listen or purchase without agreeing. We do not use advertising tags on these pages.

Your choice

Your preference is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. You can change it at any time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable optional statistics. Rejecting analytics prevents future events, but does not automatically erase information sent earlier.

External providers

Audio plays through an external provider, which receives technical information to deliver the file. If you open payments or content hosted on external platforms, their own privacy notices and cookie policies apply.

Product access and sessions

When you use the Product Center, your browser may store the necessary login session, a preference to remember this device for 24 hours, and privacy options. This login storage is not advertising tracking. Review the Product Center cookie policy. External payment providers have their own controls.
